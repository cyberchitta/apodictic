1.7. The manifest🔗

Everything the law carries is here, in one place. Nothing outside this list is assumed. The list is not compiled by reading the source. Nor is it transcribed from a command. The command runs here, and what follows is what it printed.

Read it by kind. The sort is what the audit turns on.

  • The one praxeological claim (SwapDominant) is the thing that would have to be true of all action for the law to follow.

  • The situational conditions are what an Austrian points at to say the law did not apply here. They are three of the five set out above: independence of uses (IndependentUses agent time), interchangeability of units (plan.Homogeneous) and one more unit (stock.OneMore). The rest is bookkeeping. It fixes which two supplies are being compared.

  • The condition on the data says only that two wants can be told apart.

  • The conditions carried by the vocabulary are not binders at all. They are fields of the structures the theorem takes as arguments. So whoever supplies a stock and a plan has already discharged them. They are preconditions of using the law in every sense that matters. They are the assumptions an encoding can hide. They are set out again under The vocabulary.

The last line is Lean's own logical background. propext and Quot.sound arrive with ordinary mathematics. Mathlib's finite sets bring both. So does extensionality for sets. Classical.choice is absent. No proof here fills a gap by assuming that every question has a yes-or-no answer. The proofs do split into cases. But they split only on whether two ends are the same end. That is why being able to tell two ends apart is declared in the statement ([DecidableEq praxis.End]), instead of slipping in unnoticed.

The chain form carries the same manifest without interchangeability of units (plan.Homogeneous). The urgency principle carries the same claim and kinds of condition as the chain form. The chain form is worth printing too, because of one line in it:

#manifest derives both manifests from the theorem itself. It walks the signature and sorts every binder by kind. Then it reads one level into the vocabulary. There it reports the Prop fields of the structures the signature names. It decides that a binder is a praxeological claim when the head of its type is declared in Praxeology.lean. That is the project's own rule, asked of the compiler rather than of a maintainer. So a claim declared in the wrong place shows up as a situational condition. That is the mistake worth catching.

What #manifest will not do is sort what it finds one level down. Whether a carried condition could fail of a real situation is a judgement about the world. It is not a judgement about the term. So the two carried conditions (stock.unitsAlike, plan.servesOnlyWhatItCan) are sorted by hand under The vocabulary. That sorting is the only thing here a reader is asked to take on anyone's word. The lists themselves are printed by the compiler when this document is built. A page that showed anything else would not build.

Three things limit how much that settles. Each is load-bearing.

  • The linter set out under The claims is never switched off here. But switching it off is possible. So keeping it on is our practice, not something the machine guarantees.

  • The linter can also be silenced one binder at a time, by prefixing a hypothesis with _. Doing that is how we record that the hypothesis does nothing. marginal_utility_chain carries one such binder, _firstStep (stock.OneMore small medium). That it does nothing is itself a finding.

  • The linter judges each binder whole. So it cannot see a hypothesis half of which is used. That happens in both marginal-utility theorems.

#manifest closes the second of those. The output above is the proof of it. _firstStep is listed, and marked listed, does no work, exactly where the linter has gone quiet. That is the line the chain form was printed for.

#manifest does not close the third. A half-used hypothesis looks whole to both. And #manifest brings a limit of its own. Lean draws no line between a signature and a conclusion. So the command cuts at the first anonymous binder, and reports how many it dropped. A conclusion binder the author named, before that cut, is still listed under vocabulary. atSmaller and addedFirst above are bound in the conclusion. So is least under The ladder as a history. No claim is about them. Read the count.

So the guarantee is narrower than "nothing here is idle". It is this: nothing here is idle except where the statement says so, in the only way Lean has for saying it.
